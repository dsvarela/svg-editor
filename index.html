    Savednone

    A saved style is the four values above under a name. Click one to put it on the selected shapes, or with nothing selected to set what you draw next. It is a way of working rather than part of the drawing, so it is not in the file and not in the history: what the export carries is the style on each shape, which is what applying one puts there.

    Fill, stroke and width for the selected shapes. With nothing selected these set what the next shape you draw will look like, which records no undo step. Rule decides how overlapping subpaths fill: Nonzero fills them all, Even-odd punches holes where they overlap.

    Combineneeds 2+

    Shift-click the list to select two or more. The first survives, keeping its name and colour; Subtract takes the rest away from it.

    Make one shape changes no geometry. It puts the paths into one shape, where the Rule above decides what fills: set it to Even-odd and a path inside another becomes a hole. The booleans rebuild the outline instead, which is why none of them can make one. Split into shapes undoes it, giving each path a shape of its own. A hole does not survive the trip: it is a relationship between two paths in one shape, so splitting them makes the inner one a filled shape again.

    Draw

    Corner is the radius the rectangle tool draws with, clamped to half the shorter side. Circularise takes a hand-drawn or imported near-circle and makes it exact, keeping every node and rebuilding its handles from the arc each one now spans. Within is how far the drawing may move. At 0 it removes only nodes that are doing nothing, which cannot change the file you export, so it is safe on anything. Above 0 it also gives up nodes that carry a little of the shape. Redraw curves lets it replace your curves with fitted ones; left off, every node that survives is one you placed. Sharp corners are kept whichever you choose. Reverse turns a path round without moving it: the drawing is identical, and which way it runs decides winding under nonzero and which end a marker lands on.

    Applies to the selection, or to everything when nothing is selected.

    Select two neighbouring nodes to bend the segment between them. Ctrl+←→ bends, Ctrl+↑↓ loosens.

    Align

    Click a node to edit it. Hollow handles can be pulled out to curve a straight segment.

    Delete

    Heal keeps the path whole, so a pentagon becomes a quadrilateral. It rebuilds one segment out of two, so it approximates. Split leaves two ends and touches no surviving segment at all. Neither is Break here, which keeps the node, nor Fuse, which removes a segment rather than a node.

    Canvas

    The page the drawing sits on, and the viewBox the export writes. It is drawn on the canvas with everything outside it dimmed. It never changes on its own, so drawing outside it is allowed and stays outside it until you say otherwise.

    Angles

    The grid gives you positions; this gives you directions. With it on, the pointer is held to the nearest ray, so a 45° chamfer or a twelve-armed star is drawn rather than nudged into place.

    With no origin set the rays come from wherever the gesture started: the node you are dragging or the pen's last point. Origin here pins them to the middle of the selection instead. A ray is a line, so it is beaten by a node and it beats the grid.

    Guides

    Lines you place and then aim at. Turn the rulers on and drag one out of either; drag it off the canvas to put it away. Or type a number and press Vertical or Horizontal, which is the exact route and the only one without a pointer.

    A guide reaches across the whole view rather than stopping at the page, and the tools snap to it the way they snap to an outline. Where two of them cross they make a point, which beats either line on its own. Guides are never part of the export, and placing, moving or removing one is undoable.

    Keylines

    The icon grid: a circle, a square and two rectangles sharing a centre, in Material's proportions. A square reads heavier than a circle of the same width, so the circle is drawn larger, and drawing every icon to these keeps a set at one optical weight. The dashed square is the live area, which the drawing is meant to stay inside.

    They come from the canvas, so they are never in the export and there is nothing to move. The grid is square even when the page is not, since a stretched circle would be no use as a circle. While they are shown the tools snap to them, the same as to any other outline.

    Grid

    A stroke is painted centred on its path, so a 1-unit stroke on a whole coordinate covers half of two pixels instead of all of one. Pixel fit shifts the grid by half the stroke width to put the painted edges on whole pixels. The shift follows what you have selected, or the width you are about to draw with.

    Backdropnone

    A tracing reference, not part of the drawing. It never appears in the Shapes list and never reaches the export. Unlock it to drag it about. Loading, moving and removing are all undoable; opacity, Show and Locked are not, because they say how you want to look at it.

    Traceno image

    One shape per colour, holes and all. Colours caps the palette, Within is how far a fitted curve may move in document units, and Noise drops regions with fewer boundary points than that. The backdrop stays where it is, so you can compare. Tracing is one undo step.
